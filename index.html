About Iron Lotus

Drawn from the ash of a burning library, Iron Lotus is a blade whose petals open only in flame. Where other weapons cut, it reads — unbinding an opponent's story page by page and feeding each one to the fire. What remains is a single ember bloom, iron-hard and never cooling.
